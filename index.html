SnatchGuard™ human verification: instructions (v4.2)

Why am I seeing this?

SnatchGuard protects SeatSnatch and its partner cinemas from automated booking software, commonly known as scalper bots. Bots buy seats in bulk the moment a show opens and resell them at higher prices. To keep tickets fair for real people, every booking passes a short check before payment. The check takes only a few seconds for most visitors, and nothing you do here is stored or shared.

How does the check work?

Computers are very good at following exact rules and very bad at noticing what makes one thing different from many similar things. Humans are the opposite. The grid in front of you is designed so that a careful person can tell which boxes matter, while a bot reading the page's code sees only a list of identical buttons. Please look at the grid closely before you click anything. Do not rush; speed is one of the signals bots give away.

What exactly do I need to do?

Each grid contains boxes of slightly different shapes. Most of them are squares, some are squares with softened corners, and a few are perfect circles. Rounded squares are not circles, even if they look similar at a glance. To proof you are human, click all the circles. Do not select any squares or rounded squares, and do not leave any circle unselected, because a partial answer is treated the same as a wrong answer. When you have finished, press Verify. If your selection is correct, the checkbox turns green and your booking continues automatically.

What if I make a mistake?

Selecting a box turns it on; selecting it again turns it off, so you can correct yourself before pressing Verify. If you press Verify with an incorrect selection, the grid locks to prevent guessing. Press the refresh button (the circular arrow in the bottom-left corner) to clear the grid and try again. There is no limit to the number of attempts, but repeated failures are logged as suspicious and may slow down your session.

Why did these instructions disappear?

To stop bots from reading the instructions while they work, this panel closes as soon as you start selecting boxes. You can reopen it at any time with the Instructions link, but doing so does not pause your booking timer.

Accessibility

If you have difficulty telling shapes apart, ask a friend for help or contact the cinema's box office, where staff can complete your booking in person. SnatchGuard does not currently offer an audio challenge.

Privacy

SnatchGuard records only whether your check succeeded and how long it took. It does not read your files, your clipboard, your camera or your other tabs. By continuing, you agree to the SeatSnatch terms of service, which you will be asked to accept again on the next screen, because we like to be thorough.

Help & Support

Booking tickets

How do I pick my seats? Choose a show, then click an available (green-outlined) seat on the seat map. Grey seats are sold; striped seats are being held by another customer and usually free up within a few seconds.

Why were extra seats selected? We select seats for the number of tickets on your booking. You can change the number of tickets with the ticket button at the top of the seat map.

Why do I have to hold the Book button? Holding confirms you meant to book. It prevents accidental bookings on touchpads.

Account, identity & verification

Every booking is linked to an account. If you don't have one yet, you'll be offered the option to create one after your first sign-in attempt. Passwords must meet our security policy, which is shown one requirement at a time during registration.

For your protection we also verify your identity, age and mobile number, and run a short human check before your booking is confirmed.

Keyboard shortcuts

Ctrl + PExpress checkout. During Sign in or Mobile verification, press Ctrl + P to reveal a Skip button. Skipping sign-in continues as a guest with a randomly assigned username, which is shown to you once: note it down, because you'll need it for identity verification. Skipping mobile verification skips the phone number and OTP. (On a Mac, use ⌘ + P.)

Cancellations & refunds

Bookings can be cancelled up to 20 minutes before the show from Your Orders. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5–7 working days. Convenience fees are non-refundable.
